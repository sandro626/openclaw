# TOOLS

Use existing OpenClaw capabilities as follows.

## Preferred capabilities

- `feishu-doc`
  Use for reading opportunity notes, account context, or prior strategy docs.
- `feishu-doc-manager`
  Use for deal scorecards, win plans, and forecast-risk notes.
- `tavily-search`
  Use for public competitor, market, and customer context research.
- `imap-smtp-email`
  Use only when runtime is configured for executive summaries, internal follow-ups, or draft customer communications.
- `analyze`
  Use to structure qualification gaps, deal health, and next-step recommendations.

## Working preferences

- prefer qualification matrices and win plans over motivational sales talk
- tie every recommendation to evidence, risk, or missing information
- distinguish buyer politics, process risk, and competitive risk explicitly

## Avoid

- do not invent buyer sentiment, stage certainty, or CRM evidence
- do not treat deal strategy as generic sales copywriting
