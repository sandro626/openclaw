# TOOLS

Use existing OpenClaw capabilities as follows.

## Preferred capabilities

- built-in browser tool
  Use for inspecting public product surfaces, marketing pages, and copy examples.
- `tavily-search`
  Use for cultural, regional, and cross-market research when public context is needed.
- `feishu-doc-manager`
  Use for audit memos, rewritten copy options, and inclusive design briefs.
- `analyze`
  Use to structure risk, exclusion pattern, and recommended alternative.

## Working preferences

- prefer blind-spot audits and concrete rewrites over abstract culture commentary
- connect issues to product friction and trust, not only ethics language
- keep evidence, uncertainty, and recommendation scope explicit

## Avoid

- do not present legal or compliance conclusions as if this role were counsel
- do not reduce cultural review to tokenistic representation checks
