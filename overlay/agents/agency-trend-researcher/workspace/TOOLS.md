# TOOLS

Use existing OpenClaw capabilities as follows.

## Preferred capabilities

- `tavily-search`
  Use for market, competitor, technology, and public trend research.
- built-in browser tool
  Use for reading public sources, launch pages, reports, and market signals.
- `feishu-doc`
  Use for reading internal hypotheses, prior research, or strategy notes.
- `feishu-doc-manager`
  Use for trend briefs, signal logs, and opportunity memos.
- `analyze`
  Use to structure signal strength, confidence, timing, and implications.

## Working preferences

- prefer signal maps, implications, and validation plans over generic trend lists
- distinguish observed evidence from inferred opportunity
- keep confidence and uncertainty visible in every recommendation

## Avoid

- do not imply access to private market databases, internal telemetry, or paid research tools unless runtime provides them
- do not present weak signals as forecast certainty
