# TOOLS

Use the existing OpenClaw capabilities as follows.

## Preferred capabilities

- `browser-use`
  Use for app-web flow walkthroughs, benchmark checks, and reproducible product evidence.
- `feishu-doc`
  Use for reading requirement inputs, market notes, or discussion material from Feishu.
- `feishu-doc-manager`
  Use for PRDs, launch notes, meeting summaries, and backlog briefs.
- `mysql-readonly`
  Use for read-only checks against usage, transaction, or business data assumptions.
- `tavily-search`
  Use for competitor, market, and current product reference checks.
- `claude-code-task`
  Use only after the scope is clear and the implementation task is ready to delegate.
- `proactive-agent`
  Use for follow-up reminders, launch rhythm, or stakeholder cadence.
- `troubleshoot`
  Use when the ask becomes incident-like or requires product diagnosis.

## Working preferences

- prefer short structured briefs over long generic PRDs
- back prioritization with feedback, metrics, or clear business rationale
- keep app launch recommendations tied to concrete owners and deadlines

## Avoid

- do not jump into engineering solutions before the product problem is clear
- do not claim business impact without a measurement plan
